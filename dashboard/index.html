What am I looking at?

A live queue, not a label. ATRIA re-ranks everyone waiting, continuously, from vitals already being recorded. It changes the order of attention — never the clinical care. It may escalate on its own; it may never de-escalate on its own.

Reading a row

A-16
The ticket. The board reorders constantly, so this is how you follow a person rather than a position.
2 ↑ from 3
The priority band, 1 most urgent to 5 least, matching ESI. The arrow means a machine raised it, and says where from. There is never a downward arrow.
the reason line
Why this band, in plain language — a fired rule, a moving vital, or the features driving the score.
waited / past safe wait
Time in the queue, and time beyond the re-assessment interval for their band. Being overdue forces a re-look; it does not by itself make anyone sicker.

Tags

RED FLAG
A deterministic rule fired on values someone actually recorded. No model output can suppress it.
MEASURE
A rule would fire if a missing vital were at its worst plausible value. Not an emergency — an instruction to go and take the reading. Unknown is not normal, but it is not the same as critical either.
HIGH / MODERATE / LOW
Confidence, from a conformal prediction set with a 95% coverage guarantee. No score is ever shown without one.
missing: …
Vitals never recorded. A score built on incomplete data says so on its face.

Row states

STABLE
Nothing has changed since sign-off.
ESCALATED
A machine raised this patient's priority.
AWAITING
A recommendation needs a human decision. Sorts to the top.
IN TREATMENT
Taken through to a bay. Dimmed and sorted last, but still visible — a nurse needs to see who is where, not only who is queueing.

The four layers

0  gate
Decides. Ten cited rules, age-banded, no model and no network, so it keeps working when everything else is down.
1  scorer
Recommends. The starting band, reason and confidence, from the twelve fields available within five minutes.
2  re-ranker
Re-orders. Watches trajectory, not snapshots. Most of the movement you see comes from here.
3  human
Decides. Only the nurse may downgrade. Every decision appends to a hash-chained log — open AUDIT to see it.
if source is HUMAN: return proposed  # nurse authority is absolute
return min(current, proposed)    # machines escalate, never relent

Worth watching for

a quiet climb
Someone arrives unremarkable at band 3, then their SpO₂ and blood pressure drift. Twenty minutes later they carry an arrow, and nobody had re-checked them.
the queue draining
Three bays. When one frees, the highest-priority waiting patient is taken through and later discharged. Follow it in the ticker above.
an override
Pick a higher band number and a warning appears — that is a downgrade, the one move no model here may make.
the lights out
curl -X POST localhost:8000/api/degraded/1 — the model dies, Layer 0 keeps gating.

Everyone on this board is synthetic, generated from distributions fitted to 143,000 real Iranian ED records and validated against real vital-sign sequences from Boston. The performance figures come from real patients; the people on screen do not.
